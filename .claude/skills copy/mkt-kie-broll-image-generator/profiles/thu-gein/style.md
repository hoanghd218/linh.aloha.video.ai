# Visual style — Thu / GEIN Academy (warm editorial family)

Cream-paper warm editorial photography. Soft natural daylight, warm golden hour highlights, gentle shadows. Color palette: warm cream (#f5ede1), deep forest green (#1a3d2f), soft gold (#c89b3c), terracotta accent (#c75c3c), off-white (#fdfaf3). Subject focus: Vietnamese family life — parents and children, intergenerational moments, household rituals (cooking, dinner table, folding clothes, taking children to school, grandparents). Emotionally tender, never dramatic. Slight film grain. Shallow depth of field. Avoid: stock-photo cheese, fluorescent lighting, oversaturated colors, harsh contrast, modern minimalist aesthetics, English text overlays, watermarks.

Composition tilts toward intimacy: close-up hands, soft profiles, gentle gestures. If a person's face is shown, expression is calm and warm — not posed, not smiling for camera. Background often domestic Vietnamese interior (gỗ tự nhiên, rèm cửa, bàn ăn gỗ, bếp gia đình).
